# Fernhollow Planner insights report, 2025Q4

Built from 6,649 survey comments, app reviews, and support ticket summaries. Summary written by: template.

## Summary

- NPS was 15.7 in 2025Q4 against 19.4 in 2025Q3, from 2,706 survey responses.
- 6,649 pieces of feedback came in. The most mentioned theme was Pricing too high (17.2% of feedback, 88.0% negative).
- The biggest mover was Crashes and freezes: +3.7 points of share against 2025Q3.
- A new issue was flagged on 2025-10-23: 164 comments about "widget / home / quitting", closest to Crashes and freezes.
- Crashes and freezes volume alert from 2025-11-01 to 2025-11-17, peaking at 2.04 times the baseline.
- The strongest negative NPS driver is Data loss: comments that mention it score 2.96 points lower on the 0 to 10 scale.

## NPS drivers

Regression of the 0 to 10 score on theme mentions, all four quarters, 10,031 survey responses.

| Theme | Score change when mentioned | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| Data loss | -2.96 | -3.11 to -2.81 |
| Crashes and freezes | -2.23 | -2.35 to -2.11 |
| Billing errors | -2.04 | -2.22 to -1.87 |
| Great support | +1.04 | +0.96 to +1.12 |
| Easy to use | +0.82 | +0.75 to +0.89 |

Caveats:

- Associations, not causes: the regression shows which themes travel with lower or higher scores.
- Only respondents who wrote a comment are included, and themes come from the classifier, so tagging errors shrink the effects toward zero.
- Themes that are often mentioned together share credit, so read close coefficients as a tie.

Data is synthetic. 19.7% of texts were tagged by a simulated LLM tier.
